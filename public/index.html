How DinoSwords Works

Run, survive, and rack up the highest score! Mystery boxes float across the course containing letters from A to Z. Collect them to unlock weapons ranging from Handguns and Flamethrowers to the MSCHF Helicopter, 100 Thieves Tank, and giant Meteors.

Watch out for Cacti, flying Pterodactyls, and the rare Golden Midas Pterodactyl (+50 bonus points on kill). Beware of the Double-Edged Sword (50/50 behead) and Cigarettes (11 uses triggers fatal lung cancer!).

Optimized for phones: use your left and right thumbs for Duck, Jump, and Quick Weapon taps. Scores are saved online every two seconds.
