How the round works

Everyone shares the duration chosen by the organizer, from one to five minutes. Register and mark yourself ready in the waiting room. The organizer starts a shared countdown, then the game opens for everyone. Join before the start for the full window. Jump over cacti and duck under birds. After a collision, press Jump to try again. Only your best run counts.

Your saved best is confirmed online every two seconds. A refresh, hidden tab, or long connection loss can end the current run and lose unsaved gameplay. The timer keeps running. Use the same browser to resume your entry; if browser storage is lost, ask an organizer for recovery.

Equal scores rank by the earliest time the score was achieved, then by roll number. Rankings stay provisional until the organizer freezes results. Prize winners are verified in a supervised final.
